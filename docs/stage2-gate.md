## Deviations from the PRD (with ADRs)

- DuckDB runs in the API process for bounded hot-tier aggregates instead of in a worker
  (PRD §4.1); memory, threads, byte and time budgets bound it, and large scans stay with Stage 5
  jobs (ADR-0058).
- The router labels missing coverage and creates no missing-data chunk or job (PRD §8.3 step 6 and
  §8.4 are Stage 5; ADR-0056).
- Tier labels (hot three months, 12-month window) come from the environment's reference time, not
  the wall clock, and do not drive routing because Stage 1 retains all accepted science in both
  stores (ADR-0056).
- "Indian Ocean" is the Stage 1 operational envelope and "Southern Indian Ocean" a FloatChat
  definition; the IHO polygon is offered as "Indian Ocean (IHO)", clipped (ADR-0060).
- `qc-policy-v1` fixes value selection on top of the contract's `core-good-v1` and the Argovis
  mode-to-variant rule; aggregation defaults to profile-balanced means (ADR-0057; owner to confirm).
- The development dataset is a copy of the preserved acceptance session (ADR-0059; owner to
  confirm); the session itself was started read-only and stopped.

## Known issues and risks

- The GDAC population is not served (ADR-0056); a cross-source identity rule is still open.
- `/v1/floats` returns identity and statistics derived from profiles; PRD §6.1 platform metadata
  (type, DAC) does not exist in the Stage 1 schema.
- Keyset cursors are unsigned opaque tokens; a client can forge a position but cannot read
  anything the plan would not allow.
- The DuckDB route depends on the object store for the first fill of each part; a cold request
  over the whole quarter fetches about 288 MiB (the measured cold latency includes it).
- No authentication, quotas or rate limits (Stage 7); the API binds to loopback only.
- `tests/test_refetch.py` HTTPS deadline tests are load-sensitive on this host (observed failing
  under the import and three concurrent test runs, passing in isolation).
